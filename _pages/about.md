I am a french doctoral student in applied mathematics at the SAMM laboratory in Paris 1 - Panthéon Sorbonne, under the supervision of Jean-Marc Bardet. My interests are related to concentration of measure, dependent random processes with a disrete time step (ARMA, GARCH, ...), and more generally statistics, probability, transport and entropy. I work on estimation of time delayed phenomena and how concentration appears complex settings.
