I am a french doctoral student in applied mathematics at the SAMM laboratory in Paris 1 - Panthéon Sorbonne, under the supervision of Jean-Marc Bardet. My interests are related to concentration of measure, dependent random processes, and more generally statistics, probability, transport and entropy. I work on estimation of time delayed phenomena and how concentration appears complex settings.

I also had the chance to be a part of the doctorate respresentative of the FP2M, which lead to be an organizer of biannual programm of conference reserved to doctorate and post-doctorate students, where subjects such as post-PhD carreers, mathematics and society, mathematics and tools were addressed.
