For a better understanding of the digital world we are living in, the french government finances cycles of conferences in libraries. I gave a conference on artificial intelligence, its key issues and prospects, relying on several examples illustrating some modern algorithms. The goal is to give each and everyone a glimpse of how those systems work and the potential danger of it to non professionals.

This conference was given twice, with a two years gap, the second time focusing more on generative models.
